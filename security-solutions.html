Protection in layers, watched around the clock

No single product stops every attack. We combine firewalls, endpoint protection, email filtering and identity controls, then monitor the whole environment so suspicious activity is caught and contained quickly.

We start with a review of where you're exposed today and fix the highest risks first, so your budget goes where it does the most good.

What's included

Next-Generation Firewalls (NGFW)

Fortinet FortiGate and Cisco Secure Firewall, designed around your users and applications to block sophisticated attacks.

Managed Detection & Response (MDR)

Specialists monitor your environment 24/7 and respond to threats as they happen.

Endpoint & Network Detection (EDR/NDR)

Behaviour-based monitoring of devices and network traffic to spot anomalies and isolate threats fast.

Email Security

Filtering and authentication that blocks phishing, malware and impersonation before it reaches inboxes.

Identity & Access Management (IAM)

Multi-factor authentication, single sign-on and conditional access with Microsoft Entra ID, Cisco Duo or FortiAuthenticator.

Secure Access Service Edge (SASE)

SD-WAN and zero-trust security delivered from the cloud, protecting users wherever they work.

Intrusion Detection & Prevention (IDPS)

Inspects network traffic and automatically blocks known attack patterns.

Security Information & Event Management (SIEM)

Collects and analyses logs across your environment for faster detection and investigation.

Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

Classifies sensitive information and stops it leaving the business by accident or on purpose.

Web Security

Filters web traffic and blocks malicious sites, downloads and risky applications.

Network Security

Hardening, policy and monitoring across your network to reduce the attack surface.

DDoS Protection

Mitigation services that absorb denial-of-service attacks and keep your services online.
