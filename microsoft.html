Microsoft questions

Which Microsoft 365 plan is right for our business?

For most businesses with up to 300 users, Microsoft 365 Business Premium is the strongest value because it includes Intune, Defender for Business and Entra ID P1 alongside the Office apps. We'll check what you actually need before recommending a change.

Can you migrate us to Microsoft 365 without downtime?

In most cases, yes. We migrate in stages, sync mail and files in the background, and switch over outside business hours so staff start the next day with everything in place.

Can you help us align with the Essential Eight?

Yes. Many Essential Eight controls, such as multi-factor authentication, application control, patching and restricting admin rights, can be delivered with Microsoft 365, Intune and Defender. We assess where you are and close the gaps.

Are we ready for Microsoft 365 Copilot?

Copilot can surface anything a user has access to, so overshared files become a real risk. We review permissions and sensitivity labels first so the rollout is safe.
