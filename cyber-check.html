    Frequently asked questions

    What is the Essential Eight?

    The Essential Eight is a set of eight baseline cyber security strategies published by the Australian Signals Directorate (ASD). It's widely used by insurers, government and larger clients as a measure of whether a business has the basics in place.

    Is this a formal Essential Eight assessment?

    No. It's a quick self-check based on Maturity Level One of the Essential Eight, designed to show where the biggest gaps are. A formal assessment involves checking configurations and evidence on your systems, which we can do for you.

    Is the Essential Eight being replaced?

    The ASD has announced it will gradually move from the Essential Eight to broader guidance called Essentials over the next few years. The eight controls remain the recommended baseline in the meantime, and fixing them now puts you in a strong position for whatever comes next.

    What happens after I send my results?

    One of our engineers will review your answers and get back to you, usually within one business day, with a short summary of what to fix first. There's no cost and no obligation.
