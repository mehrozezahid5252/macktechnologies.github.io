One security platform from the office to the cloud

Fortinet's strength is that the firewall, switching, Wi-Fi, SD-WAN and remote access all work as one platform. Set up well, that means fewer consoles, consistent policy and faster answers when something looks wrong.

We size the right FortiGate for your sites, build clean and documented policy, keep firmware current, and monitor logs so issues are caught early.

Fortinet questions

Can you take over management of our existing FortiGate?

Yes. We start with a configuration and health review, document what's there, tidy up risky or unused rules and bring firmware up to date. From there we can manage it ongoing or support your team as needed.

Do you offer managed Fortinet firewalls?

Yes. Managed firewall services cover monitoring, firmware updates, policy changes, log review and reporting, either as part of a wider managed services agreement or on their own.

Do we need SD-WAN?

If you have more than one site, rely on cloud applications, or want a backup internet link that fails over automatically, SD-WAN is usually worth it. FortiGate includes SD-WAN, so many businesses can turn it on without new hardware.

What's happening to FortiGate SSL VPN?

Fortinet is moving remote access away from SSL VPN tunnel mode towards IPsec VPN and zero-trust network access (ZTNA) in newer FortiOS releases. We can review your current setup and plan the change so staff aren't disrupted.
