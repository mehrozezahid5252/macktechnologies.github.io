Cisco questions

Should we choose Cisco Catalyst or Meraki?

Meraki suits businesses that want simple cloud management across several sites. Catalyst suits larger or more complex networks that need fine-grained control. We'll recommend based on your size, in-house skills and budget.

Can you support our existing Cisco network?

Yes. We review and document what you have, check firmware and support contracts, fix anything risky, and then support it ongoing.

Can Cisco and Fortinet work together?

Yes. A common design pairs Cisco switching and Wi-Fi with a Fortinet firewall. We design both so they work cleanly together.

Can you help with Meraki licence renewals and hardware refreshes?

Yes. We track renewal dates and end-of-life notices and plan replacements before they become urgent.
